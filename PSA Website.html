Where science
meets therapeutic reality

Founded in 2007, Polovina Scientific Advisory was established by scientists who recognized that cell and gene therapy required a different kind of advisory firm — one built from the inside out, by people who have lived the science, navigated the regulatory frontier, and understand what it takes to bring a transformative therapy to patients.

"Our mission is to accelerate the development of safe, effective cell and gene therapies by providing the scientific and strategic counsel that developers need to make the right decisions at every stage of the journey."

Our Story

Born from the science,
built for the field

Polovina Scientific Advisory was founded at a moment when cell and gene therapy was transitioning from theoretical promise to clinical reality. Our founders — researchers, former FDA reviewers, and clinical development veterans — saw that the companies pioneering this space needed advisors with genuine, first-principles expertise, not consultants extrapolating from other therapeutic modalities.

Over nearly two decades, we have supported more than 150 CGT programs across viral gene therapy, ex vivo cell therapy, in vivo genome editing, and RNA-based approaches. We have been present at critical inflection points: first-in-human decisions, agency interactions on novel endpoints, CMC strategy for next-generation manufacturing platforms, and post-approval safety surveillance.

Our advisors maintain active research and regulatory roles, which means our guidance reflects not just historical precedent but the leading edge of where the science and regulatory science are moving.

Our Values

What we stand for

Patient-Centered Science

Every program we support ultimately serves patients with serious or life-threatening conditions. That reality anchors every scientific and strategic recommendation we make.

Regulatory Integrity

We help clients engage with regulatory agencies transparently and constructively. We do not advise on how to obscure risk — only how to characterize and mitigate it honestly.

Scientific Independence

We are not beholden to any platform technology, CDMO, or vendor. Our recommendations are based solely on the science and the specific context of each program.

Honest Risk Communication

Cell and gene therapies carry real uncertainties. We help clients understand and communicate those uncertainties clearly — to agencies, investors, and most importantly, patients.

Capabilities

Scientific counsel
at every stage

Cell and gene therapy development demands expertise that spans molecular biology, clinical medicine, manufacturing science, and regulatory strategy — simultaneously. Our capabilities are built around this reality, covering every critical discipline from IND-enabling research through post-approval commitments.

01
Regulatory Science & IND / BLA Strategy

End-to-end regulatory strategy for cell and gene therapy programs before FDA CBER, EMA CAT, MHRA, and other global competent authorities. We advise on IND structure, pre-IND meeting preparation, Breakthrough Therapy and RMAT designation strategies, PRIME designation in Europe, and BLA/MAA preparation. Our former agency reviewers provide unique insight into how submissions are evaluated internally.

IND / IMPD PreparationBLA / MAA StrategyRMAT & Breakthrough DesignationPRIME DesignationPre-IND Meeting PrepAccelerated Pathways
02
CMC & Manufacturing Advisory

Chemistry, manufacturing, and controls strategy for viral vectors (AAV, lentivirus, adenovirus), ex vivo cell therapies (CAR-T, TCR-T, NK cells), and in vivo delivery platforms including LNPs. We advise on process development, scale-up, comparability, release testing, and the CMC sections of regulatory submissions — with particular expertise in the analytical characterization of novel biologics.

AAV & Lentiviral Vector CMCCell Therapy ManufacturingProcess ComparabilityAnalytical CharacterizationRelease Assay DevelopmentLNP Formulation Strategy
03
Nonclinical Safety & Toxicology

Study design and data interpretation for nonclinical safety programs supporting CGT INDs. We advise on genotoxicity and insertional mutagenesis risk, biodistribution and shedding studies, immunogenicity assessment, and the application of ICH S12 guidelines for gene therapy nonclinical packages. We help sponsors determine what studies are scientifically necessary versus those that add cost without informing safety.

Genotoxicity & ICH S12Biodistribution StudiesImmunogenicityNonclinical Study DesignShedding AssessmentRisk-Based Approaches
04
Clinical Development Strategy

Trial design and evidence generation strategy for first-in-human through pivotal CGT studies. We advise on dose selection, patient selection and eligibility criteria, biomarker strategy, primary and secondary endpoint development, long-term follow-up requirements, and the design of adaptive and platform trials. We have specific expertise in rare disease orphan indications and oncology cell therapy development.

First-in-Human Trial DesignEndpoint StrategyBiomarker DevelopmentLong-Term Follow-UpAdaptive Trial DesignOrphan Drug Strategy
05
Genome Editing Scientific Advisory

Specialized scientific guidance for programs based on CRISPR-Cas9, base editing, prime editing, zinc finger nucleases, and TALE nucleases. We advise on off-target characterization strategies, delivery modality selection (viral, LNP, RNP), editing efficiency and persistence assessment, and the translational development of in vivo editing programs — a rapidly evolving area requiring current, field-leading expertise.

CRISPR / Cas9 ProgramsBase & Prime EditingOff-Target CharacterizationIn Vivo Editing StrategyDelivery Platform SelectionTranslational Development
06
Reimbursement & Market Access Science

Scientific and evidence-generation support for the health technology assessment and reimbursement challenges unique to one-time CGT products. We advise on durable efficacy evidence strategies, outcomes-based contracting frameworks, real-world evidence collection, and the design of registries and long-term follow-up programs that satisfy both regulatory and payer requirements simultaneously.
